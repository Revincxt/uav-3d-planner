# v0.4 predictive space-time experiment

## Material Passport

- Origin: experiment-agent plan adapted to the implemented v0.4 protocol
- Verification status: `PREDICTIVE_DEMO_NON_CONFIRMATORY`
- Version label: `predictive-space-time-v1`
- Date: 2026-08-05

## Scope

Version 0.4 compares snapshot-only reactive replanning with a deterministic planner that can use the
complete declared obstacle schedule. It retains the v0.3 spherical vehicle, constant-speed motion,
temporary cylinders, piecewise-linear moving spheres, and exact continuous space-time execution
audit. It does not model acceleration, attitude, wind, sensing error, forecast uncertainty, energy,
or multiple controlled aircraft.

The study asks two diagnostic questions:

1. Can complete deterministic look-ahead avoid a late hold, detour, or no-safe-action outcome that
   appears under snapshot-only planning?
2. When D* Lite's graph and endpoint contract are held fixed, how much algorithm-specific work is
   retained by state reuse relative to rebuilding the planner at every epoch?

The recorded bundle is not powered and does not rank planners.

## Frozen scenario cohort

`build_predictive_cohort()` accepts scenarios using constructor and metadata contracts before any
planner is invoked. Planner outcomes cannot affect acceptance. The fixed cohort contains:

| Cohort | Scenario | Advance decision represented |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Calibration | `wait-then-straight` | Wait for the only passage to open |
| Demo | `periodic-traffic` | Enter one of several future crossing gaps |
| Diagnostic | `closing-window` | Leave a route before its direct window closes |
| Diagnostic | `chained-restrictions` | Account for two overlapping restrictions |
| Diagnostic | `multi-obstacle` | Coordinate two crossings and a temporary zone |
| Diagnostic | `vertical-time-window` | Select an altitude before the open band changes |

All six scenario IDs, case numbers, cohort labels, and semantic fingerprints are fixed in tests for
this source revision. They were not preregistered in an earlier immutable revision and must not be
described as held-out evidence. The manifest identity `requested = accepted + rejected` is enforced.

## Planner conditions

- **Repeated 3D A\***: cold-start 26-connected planning on the current static snapshot.
- **D* Lite (reset)**: a fresh D* Lite instance at every replanning epoch.
- **D* Lite (state reuse)**: retains `g`, `rhs`, and queue state across compatible snapshots.
- **4D Space-Time A\***: searches `(voxel, time-step)` states using six fixed-speed spatial actions
  plus an explicit wait action and the complete deterministic schedule.

Reset and reuse D* Lite form the controlled state-reuse ablation. Their spatial graph, anchors,
heuristic, tie breaking, event stream, and safety audit are identical. Space-Time A* answers the
broader information-model question, but it simultaneously changes graph connectivity, temporal
discretization, planning frequency, safety-gate behavior, and budget scope. Its contrast with
reactive planners describes the complete protocols and must not be described as a pure causal effect
of forecast access.

## Frozen public protocol

- reactive simulation step: `1.0 s`;
- reactive replanning interval: `4.0 s`;
- Space-Time A* temporal resolution: `0.5 s`;
- spatial resolution: `4 m`;
- cruise speed: `8 m/s`;
- mission, prediction, and planning horizon: `60 s`;
- reactive work cap: `120,000` algorithm-specific expansions or queue pops per replan;
- predictive work cap: `120,000` expanded space-time states per mission search;
- deterministic tie breaking and no wall-clock stop in recorded mission outcomes.

Every movement and wait is independently checked with the exact continuous space-time predicate.
Space-Time A* is earliest-arrival only on its declared spatial/time lattice; it is not claimed to be
globally optimal in continuous state-time space.

## Recorded outcomes

The bundle records mission success, explicit failure reason, arrival time, movement time, total
stationary time, executed length, path excess, replans, algorithm-specific work, and safety
violations. Work units remain separate:

- expanded spatial nodes for repeated A*;
- queue pops for both D* Lite conditions;
- expanded space-time states for Space-Time A*.

`waitTimeS` is total stationary time. It includes both deliberate forecast-aware waits and short
time-lattice alignment waits caused when an exact endpoint connector arrives between two time
layers. Each public wait interval labels its reason; total stationary time must not be interpreted
as policy waiting alone. Minimum separation is currently `null` because exact collision
certification and sampled clearance are intentionally separate measurements.

## Analysis boundary

The scenario fingerprint is the independent unit. Frames and replanning epochs are repeated states
within one mission, not samples. The four curated diagnostic scenarios may be shown as paired
descriptive contrasts, but they cannot support a population claim, p-value, or reliable confidence
interval. Failures and timeouts remain in the data; successful-path metrics condition on success.

The public nominal matrix contains `6 scenarios × 4 planners = 24` deterministic missions. Temporal
resolution sensitivity at `0.5/1/2 s`, isolated-process latency, a larger independently generated
cohort, and common primitive counters remain prospective follow-up experiments rather than silently
claimed v0.4 evidence.

## Reproducibility gates

- repeat execution at one source revision must reproduce all non-timing fields exactly;
- every run ID hashes the semantic scenario, planner, protocol, and parameters;
- public floating-point values are rounded to 12 decimal places at serialization;
- every timed movement and wait is independently collision-audited;
- JSON, CSV, and scenario manifest identities are cross-checked in Python and JavaScript;
- every downloadable artifact carries its byte size and SHA-256 digest;
- no scenario is filtered by planner outcome.

The release is accepted when these contracts hold, not when a particular planner wins.
